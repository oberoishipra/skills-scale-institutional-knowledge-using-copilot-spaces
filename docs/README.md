# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This folder contains the core project management process documents used by OctoAcme. Together, these guides define how the team initiates work, plans delivery, executes against milestones, manages risk and communication, releases changes, and improves over time.

## Project Management Processes

OctoAcme follows a repeatable lifecycle that starts with project initiation and stakeholder alignment. During initiation, teams confirm the business need, define a project one-pager, identify stakeholders, clarify success metrics, and decide whether to move forward into planning. The goal is to create a shared understanding of the problem, expected outcome, and initial constraints before committing resources.

Once approved, work moves into planning and backlog creation. The team holds a kickoff, prioritizes the work, estimates scope, identifies dependencies, defines the Definition of Done, and creates a release plan with milestones. This helps the team break work into shippable increments, reduce ambiguity, and align delivery with the expected outcome.

Execution focuses on delivering value in a structured and measurable way. Teams use a project board to track work from backlog through review and QA to completion. Delivery is coordinated through standups, sprint reviews, and weekly syncs that surface progress, blockers, and cross-team dependencies. In parallel, Product and Project Managers maintain alignment on priorities, success metrics, and stakeholder communication.

Risk management and communication are treated as ongoing responsibilities rather than one-time tasks. Teams maintain a risk register, monitor dependencies, escalate blockers appropriately, and share regular updates with stakeholders through a single source of truth. Communication cadences include daily standups, weekly project syncs, milestone reviews, and incident updates when needed.

Release and deployment are standardized to reduce operational risk and improve reliability. Before a change reaches production, teams verify that acceptance criteria are met, CI and security checks are passing, release notes are prepared, and rollback plans are documented. Deployment is followed by smoke tests and post-release verification to confirm that issues are caught quickly and response actions are clear.

After each sprint, release, or major milestone, OctoAcme emphasizes retrospectives and continuous improvement. Teams document what went well, what should improve, and which actions need ownership and follow-up. These lessons are tracked in the project backlog or issues to convert feedback into concrete improvements over time.

The role model used across the project docs clarifies responsibilities and decision-making. Developers focus on building, testing, and improving software. Product Managers define value and prioritize work. Project Managers coordinate planning, risks, and communications. Stakeholders provide input and approvals. QA validates quality and acceptance criteria. Together, these roles support transparent delivery and a shared operating rhythm.
